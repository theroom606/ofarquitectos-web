Among the redwoods.

Casa Mallarauco
Mallarauco, Chile
Private house
Architecture
Francisco Cepeda + Álvaro Ramírez
Design year
2016
Designed area
550 m²
Photography
Pablo Casals

A house that preserves the woodland.

Separate pavilions preserve the existing redwoods, linked by an outdoor walkway through the trees. Prefabricated timber construction reduces disturbance to the site, while dark cladding settles the house into the woodland shade.
