From the biological corridor to the vineyard.

The proposal places the tasting centre in the park, between a biological corridor and views across the Caliterra valley. The route begins at the arrival area and follows walkways through the corridor before reaching the pavilion. The tasting room opens towards the vineyards; an outdoor space with a fire pit offers a pause and another place to meet the landscape.
