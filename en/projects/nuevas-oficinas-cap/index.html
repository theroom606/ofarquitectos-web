A proposal for a typical office floor.

The 2024 proposal studies the fit-out of a typical floor for Compañía Minera del Pacífico. The plan places workstations beside the façade, with meeting rooms, a café area and booths for calls and virtual meetings. Enclosed rooms and open areas offer different working conditions within the same floor.
