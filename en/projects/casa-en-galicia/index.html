A new piece beside the stone.

Casa en Galicia

The house sits in a small hillside village, ten minutes from the beach. The project intervenes in an old stone house, organised around an entry level linked by half-flights to the bedrooms above and an old stable below. The intervention recovers the house's original logic, demolishing unfortunate later additions and reorganising its spaces to suit its new residents. A new volume, built in prefabricated laminated timber, is added to this existing structure and houses the living room, dining room and kitchen. A system of openings connects this volume to the different levels of the old house, creating spatial tension between old and new. The horreo on the site, a traditional element of rural Galician architecture, is preserved and given a new use within the ensemble.
