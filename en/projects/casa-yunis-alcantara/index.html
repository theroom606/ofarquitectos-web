Towards the Andes.

Casa Yunis-Alcántara
Los Dominicos, Santiago
Private house / Built
Architecture
Ramírez Moletto Arquitectos
Years
2008–2010
Area
340 m²
Status
Built

Slope and views.

A family house that makes use of the slope and the view towards the Andes. The ground floor holds the shared spaces and the upper floor the private rooms, organised around an inner courtyard containing the stair. The main bedrooms are offset around a terrace to open up views. Exposed concrete and timber are the main materials.
