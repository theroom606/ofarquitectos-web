A campus in six phases.

The building will house twelve degree programmes and is developed in six phases. The main block, on the southern edge, frees the northern part of the site for sunlight and shelters it from the prevailing south wind. Three classroom wings extend perpendicularly, offset by half a level and linked by ramps in a central hall; between them, gardens bring light and vegetation inside.
