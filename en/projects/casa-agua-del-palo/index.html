Renovation of a house by Christian de Groote.

Renovation and extension of a house originally designed by architect Christian de Groote. The aim was to keep the generosity of the original spaces and add new rooms with a contemporary, homogeneous reading, with no visible extension. A service area and an upper floor were added, and almost every room was modified except the hall, living and dining rooms.
