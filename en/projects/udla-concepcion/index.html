Master plan. First prize in competition.

The El Boldal Campus master plan begins with an inner park: a meeting place that also provides shade, rainwater infiltration and landscape continuity. Existing and future buildings frame its edges, allowing the campus to grow in stages without losing coherence. Facing the city, El Boldal 2 forms a new frontage and an elevated entrance square; inside, a large void combines circulation with spaces for exchange and informal learning. The proposal won first prize in the competition together with MSRAA.
