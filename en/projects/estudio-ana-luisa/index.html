A residential music studio.

A freestanding pavilion in the courtyard of a house, used as a music studio. A weathering-steel volume rests on a glazed base that opens fully to the courtyard; inside, timber lining shapes the acoustics of the room.

01

Open to the courtyard.

The glass doors fold back and the studio becomes part of the courtyard and the water.

02

A round window.

A circular window on the side frames the interior from the courtyard and pool.

03

Material.

Weathering steel ages over time and responds to the existing brick wall.
