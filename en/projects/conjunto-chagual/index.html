The ensemble and its shared thresholds.

Conjunto Chagual brings together collective housing in Papudo. Terraces, outdoor circulation and entrances give depth to the façades and connect the scale of the buildings with the approach to each home.

01

The scale of the ensemble.

Overall views show how the blocks sit on the ground. Repeated floors meet façades shaped by terraces and circulation.

02

Approaching the homes.

Changes in level, walls and walkways define the entrances. These outdoor spaces form part of the daily route between the street and the homes.

03

Terraces and circulation.

Outdoor stairs trace diagonals across the façade. Elsewhere, parapets and terrace partitions establish a horizontal rhythm and different degrees of shelter.
