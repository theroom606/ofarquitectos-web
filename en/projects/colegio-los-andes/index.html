Master plan. Second prize in competition.

Master plan for Colegio Los Andes, a girls’ school founded in 1967, on a plateau site between San Damián and Las Hualtatas streets. The project reorganises the school’s access and courtyards: a new concrete façade with a skin of prefabricated timber screens regulates the climate of the grounds, a civic courtyard becomes the school’s new centre beside the existing chapel, and a new building —Edificio L— brings together the dining hall, auditorium and library. The kindergarten moves into its own pavilion.

01

The new entrance.

Facing the street, the school builds a continuous concrete façade with a skin of prefabricated timber screens that filters light and regulates the indoor climate.

02

The civic courtyard.

A civic courtyard organises the complex beside the existing chapel, with a concrete structure that frames the passage between buildings.

03

Kindergarten and sport.

The kindergarten moves into its own pavilion with rooms opening onto the play yard, and the complex adds a new sports building beside the running track.
