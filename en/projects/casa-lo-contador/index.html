Freeing the ground floor.

Renovation of a 1960s house. The proposal frees the ground floor and brings in daylight through a series of inner and intermediate courtyards.

01

Open plan.

The ground floor opens as a continuous space, with views across to the courtyards.

02

Courtyards.

Inner and intermediate courtyards bring light and planting into the centre of the house.

03

Terrace and garden.

A pergola extends the interior towards the garden.
