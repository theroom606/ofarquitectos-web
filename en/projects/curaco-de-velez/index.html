Municipal building. Public competition.

New municipal building for Curaco de Vélez, on a 75 by 40 m site facing the town’s Plaza Central in Chiloé. The project proposes one great interior, an “urban lock”: a glazed nave runs through the building and links its programmes, sheltered by a timber structure inspired by the Williche poncho. One of the two naves is translucent, a glass greenhouse that houses the auditorium and craft fair on the ground floor, with the mayor’s office and council chamber hanging from the upper level. Laminated timber, Aluzinc steel and glass articulate the structure and façade.

01

A great timber poncho.

The building’s structure is conceived as a great poncho of laminated timber: a mantle that shelters against Chiloé’s maritime climate and shapes a double-height glazed nave housing the auditorium and craft fair, with the mayor’s office and council chamber on the upper level.

02

The civic corner.

Facing the town’s Plaza Central, the building defines a recognisable corner between Francisco Bohle and Errázuriz streets, and its volume shapes a civic plaza sheltered from the prevailing winds.
