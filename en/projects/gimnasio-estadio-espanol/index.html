Bringing scattered programmes together.

Sports in this part of the club operated separately. The project brings together gym, artistic gymnastics, spa, offices, changing rooms and multipurpose rooms, and forms the main entrance to the neighbouring buildings. Its proportions follow the adjacent buildings, and its white materiality avoids competing with them.

01

A shared entrance.

The building organises movement through the club, from the fronton and basketball courts to the east to the indoor pool to the west.

02

Transparency.

Rooms are visually connected, allowing different activities and encouraging encounters between members.

03

Gymnastics and spa.

Artistic gymnastics and the spa complete a programme experienced as a continuous sequence.
