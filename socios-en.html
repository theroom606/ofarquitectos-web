Studio / OF Arquitectos

Partners

OF Arquitectos is led by its two founding partners. The studio works with a team of collaborators and in association with other offices, from Santiago to Patagonia.

Francisco Cepeda

Architect, founding partner

Architect, Universidad Central de Chile. Between 2006 and 2009 he worked in Barcelona on large-scale projects in Europe and North Africa while pursuing doctoral studies in Architectural Design at ETSAB-UPC. At OF he leads educational, institutional and residential development projects, and the studio’s BIM coordination.

Education

  • Architect, Universidad Central de Chile
  • PhD candidate in Architectural Design, ETSAB-UPC, Barcelona. Research proficiency with the thesis “The houses of Jorge Elton: deployment and discipline of form” (2009)
  • International Master in BIM Management, Zigurat Global Institute of Technology (2021)
  • Diploma in Real Estate Project Evaluation and Management, Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez (2014)

Teaching

  • Design studio professor, School of Architecture, Universidad de Talca (since 2017)
  • Design studio professor, Universidad Diego Portales (2011–2017): vertical studios, competition studios and thesis projects
  • Faculty, International Master in BIM Management, Zigurat Global Institute of Technology
  • He has also taught at Universidad Andrés Bello and Universidad Mayor

Before OF

  • Patrick Genard y Asociados, Barcelona (2008–2009): Mediacomplex building in the 22@ district, with Carles Ferrater (Barcelona City Award; mention at the 10th Spanish Biennial of Architecture and Urbanism), and Sofitel and Barceló hotels in Casablanca, Morocco
  • Principia Design, Barcelona (2006–2008): corporate offices for Isbank (Turkey), Banco Santander and Banesto
  • Elton + Léniz Arquitectos, Santiago (2004–2006)

Álvaro Ramírez

Architect, founding partner

Architect, Universidad de Chile, graduated with highest distinction. Before founding OF he won the first architecture prize at the Bienal del Sur with the Buchupureo House, and distinctions in public competitions at the Santiago Biennial. He has presented the studio’s work at schools and institutions in Europe and Latin America.

Education

  • Architect, Universidad de Chile, highest distinction (2003)
  • Bachelor in Social Sciences, Universidad de Chile

Teaching

  • Design studio professor, Faculty of Architecture, Art and Design, Universidad Diego Portales (since 2010)
  • Visiting professor, Centro de Estudios Superiores de Diseño de Monterrey (CEDIM), Mexico (2009)
  • He has also taught at Universidad del Desarrollo, Universidad de Chile and Universidad Mayor

Talks and exhibitions

  • “To Build an Idea”, Bergen School of Architecture, Norway (2021)
  • MEXTRÓPOLI, “Emerging contemporary Chilean architecture”, Museo de la Ciudad de México (2021)
  • “Winter: Images of contemporary Chilean architecture”, ORIS House of Architecture, Zagreb (2019)
  • Documentary “Under the winter light”, Centro Cultural La Moneda (2018)
