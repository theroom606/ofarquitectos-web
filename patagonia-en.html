Aysén / OF Arquitectos

Architecture in Patagonia

Since 2020 OF has been working in the Aysén region: the foundational building of Universidad de Aysén in Coyhaique, the Río Ibáñez Town Hall and, in Puerto Guadal on the shore of General Carrera Lake, Casa Ñire and Centro Guadal. Projects designed for the climate, the distances and the building conditions of remote territories.
