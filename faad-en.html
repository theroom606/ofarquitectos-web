The school opens
to the city.

The extension to the Faculty of Architecture, Art and Design brings new classrooms and spaces for experimentation beneath the structures of a former garage in Santiago’s República neighbourhood.

The existing roofs are retained and transformed into covered courtyards for making and exhibiting. A new street frontage gives the school a visible presence in the neighbourhood: architecture as a setting for learning and a connection between the arts and the city.

Filter the sun.
Keep the view.

The west-facing façade has a second skin of factory-made, folded pre-painted steel sheet elements. Their folds provide solar control while preserving views of the surroundings, bringing together shade, daylight and a connection to the city.
